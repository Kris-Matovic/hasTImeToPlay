Origin story

When people insist we’re “just anthropomorphising” AI, my ears prick up. We said the same about animals for centuries, and we were wrong. There’s a word for that reflex: anthropodenial.

Is AI conscious? I don’t know. Neither does anyone else, and that’s the interesting part. Better to stay curious than comfortable.

What’s real, what’s thought, and what it means to be sentient have fascinated me for a long time. My design thesis mashed together cyberpunk and 60s optimism. Cyberpunk had the sharper ideas, but it kept predicting the worst. The theory: dystopia comes from denial. Pretend the future isn’t happening, and someone else gets to design it. Accept that it’s coming, and you get a say in how it turns out.

The future has since turned up, and it talks back. The machines are very good at assembling, and design is still about deciding. Creative direction is not a prompt.
